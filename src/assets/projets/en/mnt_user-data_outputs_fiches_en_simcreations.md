## The project

A website for The Sims 4 players looking for inspiration. Wheels, generators and
other tools suggest ideas and challenges that shape the way you play and accompany
every game.

## Technologies

- **Front-end**: the interface is built with React.
- **Back-end**: Node.js and Express power all the business logic.
- **Database**: MySQL.
